🍽️ Open Plates

Add it to your home screen

No app store needed — this takes about 10 seconds.
  1. Open passtheplates.app in Safari (has to be Safari, not another browser)
  2. Tap the Share icon at the bottom of the screen (a square with an arrow pointing up)
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen
  4. Tap Add in the top corner — that's it
  1. Open passtheplates.app in Chrome
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top-right corner
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (sometimes shown as Install app)
  4. Tap Add / Install — that's it
