## Cautions
- **Cottage-food rules.** The Tier 1 "to go" groups are mostly hot meals (tamales, plates). Whether those sellers are permitted depends on the state and county, and counsel has not reviewed us yet. Recommend starting with Tier 2 and shelf-stable foods, and holding the hot-meal groups until the attorney call.
- **Buyers are local.** The app shows nearby cooks, so a cook 40 miles away gets no nearby buyers. Concentrate on Oceanside / Vista / Carlsbad first, and use national baker groups only to find cooks inside that area (or to learn what works).
- Spanish and Vietnamese groups need a post in that language, ideally checked by a native speaker.

## Post drafts (edit freely; post only with admin OK)

**A. Seller recruitment (short, no links):**
> Hi everyone! I'm Michael, a founder in Oceanside, CA. I built a free app called Pass the Plates where neighbors can buy homemade food (breads, cookies, treats, meals) straight from home cooks nearby. I'm looking for a first group of cooks to try it: free storefront, you set your prices and pickup times, and I'll personally help you post your first listing. If you sell homemade food and want to be a Founding Cook, comment or message me. Admins: happy to remove this if it's not allowed here!

**B. Local buyer + seller (for Oceanside / Vista / Carlsbad groups):**
> Neighbors! What homemade food do you wish you could get nearby? I'm building a free app, Pass the Plates, to connect home cooks and bakers in North County with people who want to buy from them. I'm lining up the first bakers now. Tell me what you'd love to order, and if you bake or cook at home and want a free storefront, message me.

**C. Baker-focused (national baker groups):**
> Hi bakers! I'm building Pass the Plates, a free marketplace where home bakers sell directly to neighbors: you keep your prices and set your own pickup times, no middleman cut on your food sales. I'm looking for a few founding bakers (sourdough, cookies, brownies, granola, jams). Happy to answer questions below. Please follow your own state's home-food rules and list allergens honestly.

Keep real photos of your own face/story for credibility; add "Link in comments" and put the tagged link (`https://get.passtheplates.app/?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=oceanside-launch&utm_content={group}`) in the first comment instead of the post body.
