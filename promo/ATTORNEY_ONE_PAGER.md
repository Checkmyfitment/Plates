# Attorney call: one-page brief (1 hour)
Pass the Plates LLC (Delaware, single member, founder in Oceanside, CA). Prepared 2026-10-29. Not legal advice. Full detail: `LEGAL_REVIEW_BRIEF.md`.

## What it is
A free app where neighbors buy homemade food from home cooks. Live on iOS (Android in review). No payments in the app: buyer and cook settle up themselves. The company never touches the food, sets prices, or delivers. Cooks agree in the Terms to follow their own cottage-food rules; we do not verify permits or inspect kitchens. Launching in Oceanside / Vista / Carlsbad (San Diego County) with a small group of home bakers, about 20 cooks, budget about $1,000.

## Ranked questions (use the hour in this order)
1. **California home-food rules vs. our launch.** Which of these do cooks in San Diego County need to sell legally: cottage food operation permit (Class A / B), microenterprise home kitchen permit, or neither? Can we safely start with bakers and shelf-stable foods only? What happens to *us* if a cook sells something they are not permitted to sell?
2. **Platform exposure.** Does hosting these listings create liability for us (product liability, aiding unpermitted sales)? What should change in how we recruit, what we say in ads, and what cooks must confirm at listing time (for example: require a county permit number or an attestation)?
3. **What we may say in ads and outreach.** Is "homemade, from home bakers, from your neighbors" safe? Anything we must avoid ("safe", "inspected", "certified")? Need for disclaimers? AI-generated food images are labeled as samples; is that enough?
4. **Terms of Service fixes.** Governing law (Delaware vs California), limitation of liability, indemnification, arbitration / class waiver, and whether the clickwrap signup box is enough. Company name and operator address in Terms and Privacy.
5. **Insurance.** Do we need general or product liability coverage before scaling past the first group?
6. **Privacy.** We log visit times for signed-in users and scan messages for scam patterns (admins never see message text). Is the Privacy Policy wording enough, and do we need CCPA language or a signup notice? Before we let admins read reported chats: what notice, reason-logging and process do you recommend?
7. **Later (only if time):** subscription auto-renewal law, per-order fees, sponsored listings disclosures, food-truck permits, trademark for "Pass the Plates".
