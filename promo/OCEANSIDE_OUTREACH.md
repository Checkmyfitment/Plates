# Oceanside baker outreach kit
Drafted 2026-10-29. Launch area: Oceanside / Vista / Carlsbad. **Bakers and shelf-stable foods first** (bread, cookies, brownies, granola, jams, candy). Do not recruit hot or perishable meals (tamales, BBQ, rice bowls, dumplings) until counsel/county confirm permits.

Rules for every message: be honest it is a brand-new app, never say "certified", "inspected", "safe" or "licensed", send from your own account, and read each group's rules first. Michael approves anything before it goes out.

## 1. Where to look (all free)
- Friends, family, neighbors who bake. Ask each for 2-3 introductions.
- Facebook groups: Oceanside / Vista / Carlsbad community groups, North County home bakers, San Diego cottage food groups. Ask the admin before posting.
- Nextdoor: Food & Dining, Oceanside neighborhoods.
- Instagram: #oceansidebakery #northcountybaker #sandiegohomebaker #vistaca #carlsbadeats #sourdoughsandiego
- Farmers markets and craft fairs (Oceanside Sunrise Market, Oceanside Farmers Market, Carlsbad Village, Vista Farmers Market; check current days): talk to bakery vendors, hand out a card.
- Church, school and neighborhood bake sales.

## 2. Instagram / text DM (short)
> Hi {name}! I love your {specific item} (saw it on {where}). I'm a local founder building Pass the Plates, a free app where neighbors in Oceanside can find and order homemade food straight from home cooks. We're opening with a small group of "Founding Cooks" and I'd love to have you. It's free, you set your own prices and pickup times, and I'll help you post your first listing. Want me to send the link?

## 3. Facebook group post (get admin OK first)
> Hi neighbors! I'm Michael, a local founder in Oceanside. I'm building Pass the Plates, a free app for buying and selling homemade food (bread, cookies, treats and more) with people nearby. I'm looking for 20 home bakers to be Founding Cooks: free storefront, you set prices and pickup, and I'll personally help you post your first listing. Comment or message me if you're interested. And if you're a buyer, tell me what homemade food you wish you could get nearby!

## 4. In person (30 seconds)
> "Hi, I love your bread. I'm building a free app in Oceanside where home bakers can sell directly to neighbors. Would you want to be one of the first twenty? Here's a card with a QR code. I'll help you set up."

## 5. Reply once someone says yes
> Awesome, thank you! Download Pass the Plates here: {link with UTM}. Sign up, tap Sell, and add one item with a photo of your own food. I'm happy to do it with you over a call or in person. A few quick asks: please confirm you're following California rules for the food you sell (I can share what I know, but check your county), and list allergens honestly.

## 6. Follow-up (3 days later, once)
> Hi {name}, just checking in. No pressure at all. Happy to help you post your first listing in 10 minutes if you'd like.

## 7. Founding Cook welcome (send after first listing)
> You're live! Thank you for being one of our first cooks. Your storefront link is in the app under your profile. Share it with your followers. If anything is confusing, message me directly.

## 8. UTM link pattern
`https://get.passtheplates.app/?utm_source={channel}&utm_medium={organic|paid|dm|print}&utm_campaign=oceanside-launch&utm_content={asset}`
Channels: instagram, facebook, nextdoor, tiktok, market-card, friend-referral.
